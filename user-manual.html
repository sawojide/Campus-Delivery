1. Introduction

Welcome to the Campus Delivery System - your comprehensive platform for managing food and product deliveries within campus premises. This system connects students, delivery riders, vendors, and administrators in a seamless delivery ecosystem.

Purpose: To facilitate efficient, trackable, and secure delivery services within the campus community while providing real-time updates and secure payment processing.

2. Key Features

For Riders:

4. Getting Started

Registration Process:

1 Visit the Platform: Navigate to the Campus Delivery System homepage
2 Choose Account Type: Select whether you're a Student, Rider, or Vendor
3 Fill Registration Form: Provide required information:
  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Password (minimum 8 characters)
  • Student ID / Vendor License (as applicable)
4 Verify Email: Check your email and click the verification link
5 Complete Profile: Add additional details like delivery address, payment methods, etc.
💡 Tip: Make sure to use a valid email address and phone number as these will be used for order confirmations and notifications.

5. Student User Guide

5.1 Placing an Order

  1. Browse Vendors: Visit the homepage to see available vendors
  2. Select Vendor: Click on a vendor to view their products
  3. Choose Products: Click "Add to Cart" for desired items
  4. Review Cart: Click cart icon to review items
  5. Checkout: Click "Proceed to Checkout"
  6. Delivery Details: Enter delivery address and instructions
  7. Payment: Choose payment method and complete payment
  8. Confirmation: Receive order confirmation with tracking ID

5.2 Tracking Your Order

  • Go to "My Orders" section
  • Click on active order
  • View real-time status:
    • Order Placed
    • ✅ Confirmed by Vendor
    • 👨‍ Preparing
    • 🚴 Rider Assigned
    • 🚚 On the Way
    • ✅ Delivered

5.3 Managing Your Profile

  • Update Information: Go to Profile → Edit Details
  • Change Password: Settings → Security → Change Password
  • Saved Addresses: Add multiple delivery addresses
  • Payment Methods: Save cards for faster checkout

5.4 Writing Reviews

  1. Go to "Order History"
  2. Select completed order
  3. Click "Write Review"
  4. Rate 1-5 stars and add comments

6. Rider User Guide

6.1 Getting Started as a Rider

1 Complete Registration: Submit required documents (ID, vehicle details)
2 Wait for Approval: Admin will verify your documents (24-48 hours)
3 Set Availability: Toggle "Available for Deliveries" when ready

6.2 Accepting Deliveries

  • Notification: Receive push notification for nearby deliveries
  • Review Details: Check pickup location, drop-off, and earnings
  • Accept/Decline: Accept within 60 seconds or it goes to next rider
  • Navigate: Use in-app navigation or your preferred maps app

6.3 Updating Delivery Status

Status Action
Picked Up Scan QR code or click "Picked Up" after collecting from vendor
On the Way System updates automatically when you start moving
Delivered Click "Delivered" and optionally take photo confirmation

6.4 Earnings & Withdrawals

  • View Earnings: Dashboard shows daily/weekly/monthly earnings
  • Payment History: Detailed breakdown of each delivery
  • Request Withdrawal: Minimum withdrawal amount: 2,000
  • Payment Schedule: Withdrawals processed within 2-3 business days

7. Vendor User Guide

7.1 Setting Up Your Store

  1. Complete Registration: Submit business documents
  2. Store Profile: Add store name, description, logo, operating hours
  3. Add Products:
    • Product name and description
    • High-quality images (max 5 per product)
    • Price and category

7.2 Managing Orders

1 Receive Order: Notification sound and dashboard alert
2 Accept/Reject: Accept within 5 minutes or auto-reject
3 Prepare Order: Update status to "Preparing"
4 Ready for Pickup: Mark as "Ready" to notify rider
5 Handover: Confirm when rider picks up

7.3 Product Management

  • Add Product: Dashboard → Products → Add New
  • Edit Product: Click edit icon next to product
  • Toggle Availability: Quick toggle for in/out of stock
  • Bulk Actions: Select multiple products for batch updates

7.4 Sales Reports

  • Daily Summary: Today's orders and revenue
  • Weekly/Monthly: Trends and analytics
  • Top Products: Best-selling items
  • Customer Insights: Repeat customers, peak hours

7.5 Withdrawals

  1. Go to "Finance" section
  2. Click "Request Withdrawal"
  3. Enter amount (minimum ₦5,000)
  4. Select bank account
  5. Submit request
  6. Processing time: 3-5 business days

9. Payment System

9.1 Payment Methods

  • Credit/Debit Cards: Visa, Mastercard, Verve
  • Bank Transfer: Direct bank transfer via PayStack
  • Mobile Money: Supported mobile payment options
  • Wallet: In-app wallet for quick payments

9.2 Payment Security

🔒 Security Features:
  • PCI DSS compliant payment processing
  • SSL encryption for all transactions
  • No card details stored on our servers
  • 3D Secure authentication

9.3 Payment Flow

  1. Student places order and proceeds to checkout
  2. Selects payment method
  3. Redirected to secure PayStack payment page
  4. Completes payment
  5. Redirected back to platform with confirmation
  6. Vendor receives notification
  7. Amount held in escrow until delivery completion
  8. Platform commission deducted
  9. Remaining amount credited to vendor wallet

9.4 Refund Policy

  • Order Cancelled: Full refund within 5-7 business days
  • Wrong Order: Full refund or replacement
  • Quality Issues: Partial or full refund based on assessment
  • Non-Delivery: Full refund if order not delivered within promised time

11. Troubleshooting

Common Issues & Solutions

🔐 Login Issues

  • Problem: Can't login
  • Solutions:
    • Verify email and password
    • Check Caps Lock is off
    • Use "Forgot Password" to reset
    • Clear browser cache and cookies
    • Try incognito/private browsing mode

💳 Payment Failed

  • Problem: Payment not going through
  • Solutions:
    • Check internet connection
    • Verify card details are correct
    • Ensure sufficient balance
    • Contact your bank for card authorization
    • Try a different payment method
    • Wait 15 minutes before retrying

📱 Not Receiving Notifications

  • Problem: No push notifications
  • Solutions:
    • Check notification permissions in browser/app
    • Ensure notifications aren't blocked
    • Verify notification settings in app
    • Update to latest version
    • Re-login to refresh notification token

🚴 Order Not Assigned to Rider

  • Problem: Order stuck in "Preparing" status
  • Solutions:
    • Wait 10-15 minutes during peak hours
    • Contact vendor to confirm readiness
    • Check if delivery area is serviced
    • Contact support if delay exceeds 30 minutes

🏪 Vendor Not Accepting Orders

  • Problem: Orders being rejected or no response
  • Solutions:
    • Vendor might be offline/closed
    • Try another vendor
    • Check vendor operating hours
    • Order might be auto-cancelled after 5 minutes

App/Website Loading Slowly

  • Problem: Slow performance
  • Solutions:
    • Check internet speed
    • Clear browser cache
    • Close unnecessary tabs/applications
    • Try different browser
    • Update browser to latest version

12. Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

Q: What areas do you deliver to?
A: We deliver within the main campus and surrounding hostels. Check the delivery area map on the homepage.

Q: What are the delivery charges?
A: Delivery charges vary based on distance:

  • 0-1km: 200
  • 1-3km: ₦400
  • 3-5km: ₦600
  • Above 5km: ₦100 per additional km

Q: How long does delivery take?
A: Average delivery time is 30-45 minutes, depending on:

  • Vendor preparation time
  • Distance to delivery location
  • Current order volume
  • Weather conditions

Q: Can I schedule an order for later?
A: Yes! During checkout, select "Schedule for Later" and choose your preferred delivery time.

Q: Can I modify my order after placing it?
A: You can modify your order within 2 minutes of placement. After that, contact support immediately.

Q: What if I receive the wrong order?
A: Contact us immediately through:

  • In-app support chat
  • Phone: [Your Support Number]
  • Email: support@campusdelivery.com
We'll arrange pickup and redelivery or full refund.

For Riders

Q: How much can I earn?
A: Earnings depend on:

  • Number of deliveries completed
  • Distance covered
  • Peak hour bonuses
  • Customer tips
Average riders earn ₦15,000 - ₦40,000 weekly.

Q: Do I need my own vehicle?
A: Yes, you need a functional bicycle, motorcycle, or scooter. You must also have a valid driver's license for motorized vehicles.

Q: Can I work part-time?
A: Absolutely! Set your own schedule. Toggle availability when you want to work.

For Vendors

Q: What commission do you charge?
A: We charge a 10% commission on each order. This covers platform maintenance, marketing, and payment processing.

Q: How often do I get paid?
A: You can request withdrawals anytime (minimum ₦5,000). Payments are processed within 3-5 business days.

Q: Can I offer discounts?
A: Yes! You can create promotional codes and special offers from your vendor dashboard.
